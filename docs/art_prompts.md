# Art prompts for ChatGPT

How to use: open ChatGPT, attach `style_reference.png` (my sample of your current art), paste ONE prompt below and send it.
Save each picture it makes and drop it here in our chat. Then do the next prompt. Redo any picture you don't like; I cut and fit them.
The sky colour and the day/night fade are done in code, so they need no pictures.
