# Top-down worker ant: prompts for GPT

Attach the reference picture (the plain worker built from the kit, walking: `kit_worker_row.png`) to the FIRST message.
Do each prompt in the SAME chat, one prompt per message, one picture per message. Save each picture as a PNG with a transparent background.

---

## Prompt 1: walk pose A

```
Draw ONLY one thing: exactly 1 worker ant seen from directly ABOVE (a flat top-down, bird's-eye view, no perspective, no side view), lying flat, perfectly left-right symmetric except for the legs. The ant's body runs horizontally and its HEAD POINTS TO THE RIGHT.

Match the attached reference ant exactly in design: a chunky grey-brown ant with a ridged, rounded abdomen, a three-part thorax, thick jointed legs, a big round glossy black eye with a small white highlight, thick curved antennae and small mandibles, drawn with a bold thick near-black outline (slightly purple-black) and flat cel shading in 2-3 tones with soft rounded highlights.

From the right to the left: a head with two large dark eyes seen from above, two jointed antennae curving forward and outward, and open mandibles pointing right; a short narrower thorax in three segments; a small waist node; and a rounded oval abdomen with faint segment lines. Six jointed legs attach to the thorax and spread out to the top and the bottom of the picture (three pairs), each with visible joints (upper leg, lower leg, tiny foot claw).

WALK POSE A (alternating tripod walk): the front-top leg, the middle-bottom leg and the hind-top leg are reaching forward (toward the right), with joints bent. The other three legs (front-bottom, middle-top, hind-bottom) are planted and pushed back (toward the left), nearly straight.

Square image (1:1), at the highest resolution you can. The ant is centred, about 70% of the image width, with clear empty space around it so no leg or antenna touches the edges. Transparent background (PNG), flat colours only. Paint the body in a mid tone (no heavy dark shadows, no bright highlights painted on the body), because the game recolours each ant. No ground, no shadow, no motion lines, no text, no watermark, no other objects.
```

## Prompt 2: walk pose B (same chat, next message)

```
Now the same ant again. Keep the body, head, eyes, antennae, mandibles, thorax and abdomen exactly the same size, shape, colours and position as in the last picture. Change ONLY the legs to WALK POSE B, the opposite of pose A: the front-bottom leg, the middle-top leg and the hind-bottom leg are reaching forward (toward the right) with bent joints, and the other three legs (front-top, middle-bottom, hind-top) are planted and pushed back toward the left. Square image, transparent background, same framing and same size.
```

## Prompt 3: walk pose C, optional (same chat, next message)

```
Now the same ant again, with everything the same except the legs: pose C, the passing pose. All six legs are about halfway through their step, with their lower legs pointing roughly straight out to the top and the bottom of the picture, evenly spread. Square image, transparent background, same framing and same size.
```

## Tips

- If the body shifts or changes between pictures, say: "match the body position, size and design of the first picture exactly."
- If the ant comes out in side view, say: "no, directly from above, the way you would see an ant crawling on glass from the front of the glass".
